Four things the research keeps coming back to

No miracle products. These are the levers with real evidence behind them — the foundation everything else on this site builds on.

1

Keep them lean

The single strongest lifespan finding in dog science: in Purina’s 14-year study of 48 Labradors, dogs kept lean lived a median of 1.8 years longer and stayed healthier. You should be able to feel ribs under a thin fat layer.

2

Move every day

Muscle is a senior dog’s savings account. Daily walks, gentle hills and short play sessions protect joints, weight and brain. Little and often beats one long weekend hike — especially after age 10.

3

Test, don’t guess

From age 7–8, twice-yearly vet visits with bloodwork catch kidney, liver and thyroid trouble while it’s still cheap and treatable. Trends matter more than any single result — keep the reports.

4

Protect teeth, joints & brain

Dental disease seeds bacteria into the heart and kidneys. Joint pain quietly shrinks a dog’s world. And cognitive decline has early signs most owners miss. All three are manageable — if you start early.

The dog longevity drugs: LOY-001, LOY-002 & LOY-003

A San Francisco company called Loyal is developing the first drugs intended to extend lifespan itself — not treat a disease. Here is where each one actually stands with the FDA, in plain English.

LOY-002
FOR: SENIOR DOGS, 10+ YEARS, 14+ LB

A daily, beef-flavored pill that targets the metabolic changes of aging — designed to mimic some benefits of calorie restriction without feeding less. The lead program, and the one closest to market.

Closest to launch
  • Effectiveness (RXE)Accepted Feb 2025
  • Safety (TAS)Accepted Jan 2026
  • ManufacturingIn progress
  • Possible availabilityLate 2026–2027 (conditional)
LOY-001
FOR: LARGE & GIANT DOGS, 7+ YEARS, 40+ LB

An injectable that lowers IGF-1, the growth hormone linked to why big dogs age faster and die younger. If it works, it targets the breeds with the shortest lives — Great Danes, Bernese, Rottweilers.

FDA milestone reached
  • Effectiveness (RXE)Accepted 2023
  • FormVet-administered injection
  • StatusIn development
LOY-003
FOR: LARGE & GIANT DOGS, 5+ YEARS, 60+ LB

A daily pill version of the same IGF-1 approach as LOY-001 — same goal (slow the faster aging of big dogs), easier format for owners who prefer a tablet at home over injections at the clinic.

FDA milestone reached
  • Effectiveness (RXE)Accepted Sept 2026
  • FormDaily pill
  • StatusIn development

How we got here

2023FDA accepts LOY-001’s reasonable expectation of effectiveness — a first for a lifespan-extension claim.
Feb 2025LOY-002 clears the same effectiveness milestone for senior dogs of nearly all sizes.
Jul 2025STAY study finishes enrolling: 1,300+ dogs at 70 US vet practices — the largest trial in veterinary medicine.
Jan 2026FDA accepts LOY-002’s safety package, including data from 400+ dogs and doses up to 5× the intended strength.
Late 2026 → 2027Final manufacturing (CMC) section still in progress. Conditional approval could follow — full lifespan proof comes from STAY data over time.
Read this before you get excited: “Reasonable expectation of effectiveness” is not proof. No LOY drug is approved or on sale yet, and no completed trial has yet shown these drugs add years. Conditional approval, if it comes, means the FDA judges the drug safe and likely effective while long-term data is gathered. We track it because it’s historic — and we’ll say so plainly if the data disappoints.
