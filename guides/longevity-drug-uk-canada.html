When Will the Longevity Drug Reach the UK and Canada?

Dog longevity drugs are making headlines in America — but if you live in the UK or Canada, the natural question is: when do we get them? The honest answer is: not first, and not immediately. Here is the realistic picture.

The short version: Loyal's LOY drugs are being developed through the US FDA first. UK and Canadian approval requires separate regulators (the Veterinary Medicines Directorate in the UK, Health Canada's Veterinary Drugs Directorate in Canada), and no launch dates for either country have been announced. Expect the US to lead by years, not months — and plan around the proven basics meanwhile.

Why the US goes first

Drug developers launch where their regulatory pathway is clearest. Loyal is a US company working with the FDA's Center for Veterinary Medicine, which has already accepted the "reasonable expectation of effectiveness" for LOY-002 (Feb 2025), LOY-001 (2023) and LOY-003, and the safety package for LOY-002 (Jan 2026). Other countries will want their own reviews of the same safety and manufacturing data — and usually their own label and distribution arrangements.

What UK owners should expect

  • Regulator: the Veterinary Medicines Directorate (VMD) authorises veterinary medicines in Great Britain; Northern Ireland follows EU rules.
  • Realistic timeline: a UK authorisation would normally follow US conditional approval by at least a year or two, and only if the company applies — nothing is guaranteed.
  • Importing it yourself is not a shortcut: unlicensed veterinary medicines cannot legally be imported for personal use in the UK; ask your vet about the legal routes (including the cascade) if and when a product is authorised elsewhere.

What Canadian owners should expect

  • Regulator: Health Canada's Veterinary Drugs Directorate reviews safety, efficacy and quality for new veterinary drugs.
  • Realistic timeline: similar to the UK — a US-first launch followed by a Canadian filing, if the company chooses to pursue it.
  • Cross-border shopping is not a plan: a US prescription drug cannot simply be carried home and used legally without Canadian authorisation and veterinary involvement.

What to do while waiting

The waiting time is not wasted time. Everything in our 12-week plan — lean weight, daily movement, senior bloodwork, dental care and early detection — works today in every country, and it is exactly what will determine how much benefit any future drug can add. Ask your vet to note your interest so your clinic contacts you when authorisation news arrives, and follow our LOY tracker for every FDA milestone.
