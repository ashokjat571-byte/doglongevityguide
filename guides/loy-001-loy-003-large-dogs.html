LOY-001 vs LOY-003: The Longevity Drugs Being Built for Large Dogs

LOY-002 gets the headlines, but for owners of Great Danes, Labradors and German Shepherds, Loyal's other two candidates may matter more. LOY-001 and LOY-003 were designed specifically for the problem that defines large-breed aging: too much IGF-1, for too long.

The short version: Both drugs aim to lower the growth hormone IGF-1, which is far higher in large breeds and is linked to their faster aging. LOY-001 is a long-acting injection given by a veterinarian; LOY-003 is a daily pill with the same target. The FDA has accepted the effectiveness section of their data packages. Neither is approved or on sale yet; company timelines have pointed to 2027 for conditional approval.

Why IGF-1 is the target

Breeding dogs for size selected for growth signalling. Large breeds carry much higher IGF-1 levels than small breeds — and across species, lower IGF-1 signalling is associated with longer life. Loyal's founding argument is that the short lifespan of big dogs is, in effect, a hormone-driven condition created by breeding, and therefore something medicine can address. Our guide to why big dogs die younger explains the biology in full.

Injection vs pill: LOY-001 and LOY-003 compared

  • LOY-001 — a long-acting injectable administered at the veterinary clinic, designed for large and giant breeds. The advantage: no daily pilling, and dosing is guaranteed.
  • LOY-003 — a daily oral pill aimed at the same pathway and breeds. The advantage: it can be started, stopped and adjusted at home like LOY-002.
  • Both remain investigational. Timelines in this field slip, and conditional approval depends on the FDA accepting safety and manufacturing packages as well as effectiveness.

What large-breed owners can do today

Do not wait for a drug to start. The interventions with evidence right now — keeping a big dog lean (worth a median 1.8 extra years in the Labrador lifetime study), early senior screening from age 6–7 in giant breeds, joint protection and prompt checks of new lumps — are exactly the habits that will also determine how much any future drug can add.
